About Habit Tracker

Habit Tracker is a simple, fun, and visually appealing tool to help you build consistent habits. Using cute, rounded cards and easy-to-use forms, you can track small daily actions and celebrate your progress.

The idea is to start small, track consistently, and stay motivated with visual feedback. Whether it's drinking water, reading, or exercising, Habit Tracker keeps your goals visible and reachable.

Why we built this

  • To simplify habit tracking with minimal friction.
  • Provide an engaging, cute interface that motivates users.
  • Encourage small, daily wins that lead to long-term consistency.
  • Practice UI/UX design skills using Tailwind CSS.
